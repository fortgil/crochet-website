About the Owner

Meet Twizie,
Owner & Creator of Rosette Crochet

Twizie is the owner and creator behind Twizie | Rosette Crochet, a handmade crochet business based in Nairobi, Kenya.

Twizie creates premium handmade crochet products and custom designs for customers in Nairobi and beyond. Born out of a deep passion for needlecraft, every stitch is crafted by hand with personal care, high-grade yarn, and royal-treatment customer service.

When you place an order, Twizie handles every single detail personally to understand your style, sizing, and color preferences, delivering a piece tailored exclusively for you.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, delivery, and crochet care.

Twizie is the founder, owner, and creator behind Twizie | Rosette Crochet based in Nairobi, Kenya. Every piece is handcrafted and personally tailored by Twizie to ensure top quality, bespoke sizing, and fast delivery.

You can order ready-made items by clicking "Order Now" on any product, which connects you straight to WhatsApp. For custom designs, send your reference photos, preferred colors, and size on WhatsApp or via the contact form below.

Ready-made pieces ship immediately (1–2 days). Custom orders typically take 3 to 7 days depending on the size and complexity of the piece.

We deliver throughout Nairobi via courier (same-day or next-day delivery) and across Kenya via reliable parcel services. We accept M-Pesa payments upon order confirmation.

Hand wash gently in cool water with mild detergent. Do not wring or bleach. Lay flat on a clean towel in the shade to dry so the piece maintains its original shape and softness.
